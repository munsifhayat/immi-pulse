# IMMI-PULSE — Community Reels (the *indirect* set)

Six high-energy reels that sell the **community** without ever pitching it. No "join our community,"
no feature tour. Each one leads with a human truth about the migration journey — the wait, the milestones,
the comparison trap, grant day — and the community is the **payoff you feel**, not the thing we advertise.

Grounded in the real product (`context/community/`): Pulse Timeline, Lodgement Clubs (cohorts by subclass +
lodge-month), the Grant Wall, the "is my wait normal?" engine. Respect the s276 guardrail (share the shared
human experience + official/aggregate facts; never advise a specific case).

**Energy & delivery (all six):**
- **Voice:** Charlie (AU male), energetic tuning — `stability 0.45 / style 0.55 / similarity 0.80`. Fast, fluent,
  rising. One VO clip per line so each beat hits on the cut.
- **Music:** energetic cinematic build (ElevenLabs *"rising cinematic build, orchestral strings crescendo,
  deep brass swells, slow tension to release, building anticipation, no drums, sweeping, heroic"*), bed at
  ~0.22 under the VO. On publish, layer a **trending upbeat sound** in-app for beat-driven energy + reach.
- **Cut:** punchy, on-beat, numbers/milestones animating. Captions burned in. 12–22s, 9:16.
- **Indirect rule:** the word "community" never appears. We show the *crowd* and the *belonging*; the viewer
  concludes "I want to be in that" on their own.

Covers for all six render from the same template → `covers/comm-*.png` (props in `reel-189-remotion/props/`).
