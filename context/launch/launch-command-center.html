The one thing that makes this work

Beating the start-then-drift loop

You start things, they excite you, they fade, you jump. That's the novelty-over-completion loop — the dopamine is in starting, the value is only in finishing. You don't beat it with motivation. You beat it with a constraint.

The Rule of One

One module is in build at a time. The other two are parked — not dead, just shelved with a written note on when to resume. You may not open a parked module until the in-build one hits its Definition of Done.
What kills products

Three things each 70% done, none finished. New idea Friday → new branch Monday → old thing abandoned. The boring last 30% (moderation, payments, SEO) never gets done because it's not novel.

What ships products

The boring 30% is the only thing on the list. One module, one finish line, one launch. New ideas go to the Parking Lot and wait their horizon. Finishing is a skill — this is the reps.

Marketplace — the verified-agent directory

Fully built, hidden behind a redirect. The bridge from "should I use an agent?" (community) to a paying agent (SaaS). The hard part isn't code — it's filling it legally.

The MVP — the finish line
A directory of migration agents, seeded from the OMARA public register as clearly-labelled unclaimed factual listings, that agents can claim & verify, with a tracked lead when an applicant makes contact — legally clean and reachable from the community.
The legal way to list agents "from Google"
  • Source from the OMARA register, not Google. Facts (name, MARN, status, suburb) aren't copyrightable. That's your safe base layer.
  • Seed "unclaimed" → agent claims → verify. The pattern every defensible directory uses. Bio/photo only with the agent's opt-in.
  • Never scrape agent sites/Google for bios/photos, never cross-match sources, never imply government endorsement.
  • Monetise by listing/lead — never % of case fee. Featured ~$35/mo or lead ~$20–60. Email OMARA in writing + lawyer sign-off before bulk seeding.

SaaS — the AI consultant console

Your hardest engineering, and it's real: AI-powered enquiry → pre-case → case → document validation. Blocked on revenue. The full push comes after the community funnel feeds it.

The MVP — the finish line
One migration agent runs one real client end-to-end, gets paid, and never needs you to hand-hold. Not feature-parity with Docketwise. Not auto-filing with DHA. One agent, one client, money collected, zero founder intervention.
What wins the scope
  • AI does the boring triage. Email → classified to a subclass → pre-case drafted → outcome suggested. Real today.
  • AI document validation. Flags expiring passport, low IELTS, name mismatch before the agent does.
  • Human-in-the-loop always. AI suggests; the agent confirms. Right posture for a life-changing field — and a selling point.
  • A client portal that isn't terrible. Every incumbent's is. The bar is on the floor.
The honesty call you must make
"Collect once → auto-fill DHA forms" does not exist — it's a static demo. Lodgement is a manual date field. Recommendation: scope auto-fill OUT of v1, position as "everything up to a lodgement-ready case," and make it the marquee v2 feature once paying pilots ask. Ship truth.
